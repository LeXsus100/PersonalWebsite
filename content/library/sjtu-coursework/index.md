During my exchange period at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/), I completed several coursework projects in materials science.
Unlike the lecture notes shown [here](/library/latex-notes/#lecture-notes), these are self-contained studies developed within specific course contexts, specifically with a stronger emphasis on modelling, analysis, or literature review.

The documents below are shared as originally submitted, with only minor adjustments where necessary.

## Projects

##### Modelling the Melting Behaviour of Pure Ni, Zr, and Ni-Zr Alloys

A molecular dynamics study (LAMMPS) of melting and phase transitions in pure Ni, Zr, and Ni-Zr intermetallics, with focus on atomistic-scale thermal behaviour.
