Open with the question, observation, or problem that makes the entry worth reading.

## The short version

Give readers the useful conclusion early. A short list often works well:

- the first takeaway;
- the decision or method;
- what changes in practice.

## Context

Explain only the background needed to understand the rest.

## How it works

Break the main idea into small sections. Use images, tables, code, callouts, or attachments whenever they explain something faster than another paragraph.

## What I would change next time

Close with limits, trade-offs, or the next experiment instead of repeating the introduction.
